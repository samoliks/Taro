Who We Are

A Czech voice for the world's mountains

Founded in Prague in 2018, Taro is a non-governmental organisation dedicated to the preservation of mountain ecosystems and the living cultures that have grown around them for centuries.

We work at the intersection of environmental science, cultural heritage, and community empowerment — bridging local knowledge in the Krkonoše, Šumava, and Beskydy ranges with global mountain conservation movements.

What We Do

Three pillars of mountain stewardship

Our work spans ecology, culture, and community — because a mountain cannot be saved unless the people and traditions that define it are protected too.

🌿

Ecosystem Preservation

Restoring alpine meadows, peat bogs, and forest corridors threatened by climate change and over-tourism.

🎭

Cultural Heritage

Documenting and revitalising mountain dialects, folk traditions, architecture, and artisan crafts.

🤝

Community Education

Training local guides, schools, and municipal leaders to become long-term stewards of their mountain home.

Current Projects

Work happening right now

From the peat bogs of Šumava to the high ridges of the Himalayas, Taro's teams are on the ground.

Czechia · Ecology

Krkonoše Alpine Meadow Revival

Czechia · Culture

Our Home Mountains

Rooted in the heart of Europe

Czech mountain ranges are small by global standards but immensely rich in biodiversity and cultural identity. Taro was born here — and remains deeply committed to these landscapes.

  • Krkonoše — Bohemian Giant Mountains, UNESCO Biosphere Reserve
  • Šumava — Europe's largest contiguous forest, vital peat bogs
  • Beskydy — Carpathian foothills with Wallachian pastoral culture
  • Jeseníky — Silesian highlands and rare glacial relict species
  • Česká Švýcarsko — Sandstone canyon country of North Bohemia
