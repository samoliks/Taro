Who We Are

A Czech voice for
the world's mountains

Founded in Fryšták in 2018 and rooted in the foothills of the Beskydy range, Taro was built on a simple conviction: that mountain landscapes and mountain cultures are inseparable. Lose one and you lose the other.

We work at the intersection of environmental science, intangible cultural heritage, and community empowerment — connecting local knowledge in the Czech highlands with global mountain conservation movements from the Andes to the Himalayas.

“
A mountain cannot be saved unless the people who live in its shadow are heard.
Taro founding principle  ·  Fryšták, 2018
What We Do

Three pillars of
mountain stewardship

Our work spans ecology, culture, and community — because a mountain cannot be protected unless the people and traditions that define it are recognised and valued.

01

Ecosystem Preservation

Restoring alpine meadows, peat bogs, and forest corridors threatened by climate change and unsustainable land use. We work alongside ecologists, land managers, and local farmers to rebuild what has been degraded.

02

Cultural Heritage

Documenting and revitalising mountain dialects, pastoral traditions, vernacular architecture, and artisan knowledge. We believe culture is not a record of the past — it is a living system that must be actively sustained.

03

Community Stewardship

Training local guides, educators, and municipal leaders to become long-term stewards of their mountain home. Lasting conservation only works when the people closest to the land are at its centre.

Our Home

Rooted in
Moravian hills

Based in Fryšták — a small town in the Zlín region at the foot of the Beskydy range — Taro was shaped by the landscapes immediately outside its door. Czech mountain ranges are modest in elevation but immensely rich in biodiversity, folklore, and identity.

  • IKrkonoše — Bohemian Giant Mountains, UNESCO Biosphere Reserve
  • IIŠumava — Europe's largest contiguous forest, vital peat bogs
  • IIIBeskydy — Carpathian foothills with Wallachian pastoral culture
  • IVJeseníky — Silesian highlands and rare glacial relict species
  • VČeská Švýcarsko — Sandstone canyon country of North Bohemia
The Founders

The people
behind Taro

Daniel Štorcel

Co-Founder & Director

Daniel grew up between Fryšták and the Beskydy trails above it. A trained ecologist with a decade of field experience across Central European mountain ranges, he leads Taro's conservation science and international partnerships. His work is guided by the belief that ecology and culture are two languages describing the same place.

Samuel Holík

Co-Founder & Head of Culture

Samuel is a cultural anthropologist and documentary maker from the Zlín region with a longstanding focus on intangible heritage in mountain communities. He leads Taro's archival, education, and community engagement programmes — and has spent years recording disappearing dialects and traditions across Moravia and beyond.
